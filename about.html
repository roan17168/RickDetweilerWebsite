About the Author

Richard A. Detweiler

Social psychologist, college president emeritus, and researcher asking what parts of college still matter decades later.

Richard A. Detweiler, known to colleagues and students as Rick, is a social psychologist, a former professor and college president, and a researcher whose work asks a plain question: which parts of college still matter decades later?

Rick is Managing Director of HigherEdImpact, a nonprofit he founded to help institutions use evidence to fulfill their educational missions. He is President Emeritus of the Great Lakes Colleges Association (GLCA) and of Hartwick College. At the GLCA he founded the Global Liberal Arts Alliance, a partnership of 30 universities in 16 countries.

Early in his career at Drew University, he led the first initiative to provide a computer to every student at a liberal arts institution, a commitment he continued as President of Hartwick College. His book, The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs, was named by Forbes as one of the best higher education books of 2021. It reports a landmark study linking specific college experiences to how graduates' lives turn out.

Rick speaks at colleges and universities and advises presidents and boards in the United States and abroad.

The Book

The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs

Lives of Consequence, Inquiry, and Accomplishment (MIT Press, 2021)

Named by Forbes as one of the best higher education books of 2021, this book provides the first comprehensive empirical study examining how specific college experiences relate to leadership, civic involvement, continued learning, cultural involvement, fulfillment, and personal success decades after graduation.
