Richard A. Detweiler

Social Psychologist • Higher Education Leader • Author

For more than five decades, "Rick" Detweiler has been a leading voice for the empirical defense of the liberal arts — moving the conversation from belief to evidence.

Richard A. Detweiler — known to colleagues and students as Rick — is a social psychologist, veteran higher education leader, and pioneering researcher whose work has redefined how we measure the value of a college degree. With a career spanning over five decades, Rick brings the unique perspective of a former college president, professor, and global consultant to the urgent conversation about the future of the liberal arts.

Rick currently serves as the Managing Director of HigherEdImpact, a nonprofit organization he founded to help institutions use evidence-based practices to fulfill their educational missions. He is also President Emeritus of both the Great Lakes Colleges Association (GLCA) and Hartwick College. At the GLCA, he founded the Global Liberal Arts Alliance, a coalition of 30 universities across 16 countries dedicated to strengthening liberal arts education worldwide.

His leadership has always been defined by innovation and a "human-first" approach. Early in his career at Drew University, he led the first initiative to provide computers to every student at a liberal arts institution — a commitment to technological integration he continued as President of Hartwick College. However, his most enduring legacy is his shift from "what we believe" to "what we know" regarding the liberal arts. His landmark study, The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs, was recognized by Forbes as one of the "Best Higher Education Books of 2021" for providing the first large-scale empirical proof that a liberal arts experience leads to more successful, altruistic, and fulfilling lives.

Whether speaking at universities about the future of the liberal arts or advising international boards, Rick remains a warm and accessible advocate for an education that prepares students not just for their first job, but for a life of consequence.

Career Milestones

  • President Emeritus, Hartwick College — Served as the 10th President, where he championed institutional innovation and relationship-rich learning.
  • Founder, HigherEdImpact — Established a nonprofit network translating research into actionable strategies for college leaders and policymakers.
  • Founder, Global Liberal Arts Alliance — Created a worldwide network of 30 institutions in 16 countries to advance the liberal arts tradition globally.
  • Author of a "Best Higher Education Book" (Forbes, 2021) — His MIT Press volume was distinguished as a definitive source on the empirical value of the liberal arts.
  • Distinguished Fellow, CLIR — Served as interim president and fellow at the Council on Library and Information Resources.
  • Peace Corps Veteran — Served as a volunteer and trainer in the Eastern Caroline Islands, shaping his global perspective on community and education.

The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs

Lives of Consequence, Inquiry, and Accomplishment

Drawing on interviews with more than 1,000 college graduates across a 50-year span, this groundbreaking MIT Press study moves the defense of the liberal arts from philosophical rhetoric to rigorous data. Rick systematically analyzed how specific college experiences correlate with long-term life outcomes — discovering that educational context often matters more than a student's major in predicting leadership, civic engagement, and personal fulfillment later in life.

It is the definitive resource for anyone needing to prove that the liberal arts are a practical necessity for a flourishing society.
