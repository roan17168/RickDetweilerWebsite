[1886] Biography
[1887] Richard A. Detweiler, known to colleagues and students as Rick, is a social psychologist, a former professor and college president, and a researcher whose work asks a plain question: which parts of college still matter decades later?
[1888] Rick is Managing Director of HigherEdImpact, a nonprofit he founded to help institutions use evidence to fulfill their educational missions. He is President Emeritus of the Great Lakes Colleges Association and of Hartwick College. At the GLCA he founded the Global Liberal Arts Alliance, a partnership of 30 universities in 16 countries.
[1889] Early in his career at Drew University, he led the first initiative to provide a computer to every student at a liberal arts institution, a commitment he continued as President of Hartwick College. His book, The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs, was named by Forbes as one of the best higher education books of 2021. It reports a large study linking specific college experiences to how graduates' lives turn out.
[1890] Rick speaks at colleges and universities and advises presidents and boards in the United States and abroad.
[1891] About the author and the study. The author is a graduate of a liberal arts college and of a major research university. He led liberal arts organizations for many years. The study included graduates of every kind of college—big and small, private and public, selective and non-selective, research and liberal arts and comprehensive, regional and national—and its findings held across all of them.
[1892] Career milestones
[1893] President Emeritus, Great Lakes Colleges Association. Led the consortium of liberal arts colleges for 14 years.
[1894] President Emeritus, Hartwick College. Served as the college's 10th president.
[1895] Founder, HigherEdImpact. A nonprofit that turns research into practical steps for college leaders and policymakers.
[1896] Founder, Global Liberal Arts Alliance. 30 institutions in 16 countries.
[1897] Author, The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs (MIT Press, 2021). A Forbes best higher education book of 2021.
[1898] Distinguished Fellow, Council on Library and Information Resources. Also served as interim president.
[1899] PhD in Social Psychology, Princeton University.
[1900] Peace Corps volunteer and trainer, Eastern Caroline Islands.
[1901] The book
[1902] Image: book cover
[1903] The Evidence Liberal Arts Needs: Lives of Consequence, Inquiry, and Accomplishment
[1904] Based on interviews with 1,000 college graduates, ages 25 to 65, the book examines how specific college experiences relate to leadership, civic involvement, continued learning, cultural involvement, fulfillment and success in adult life. Its central finding is that what students experience in college often matters more than what they major in.
