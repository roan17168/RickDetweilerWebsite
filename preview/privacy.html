Privacy

What this site does with your information

Short version: it does not ask who you are, it does not measure you, it does not sell anything about you, and it does not follow you around the web.

Effective November 2026

The short version

This is a public education site. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no comments, no advertising and no paywall. You can read everything without telling us anything about yourself.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not build profiles of individual visitors. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site trackers.

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party code

This site does not currently run analytics of any kind. There is no tracking script, no advertising pixel and no social media widget on these pages.

The site sets no cookies. Its fonts, styles and scripts are served from this site rather than from a third-party service, so reading a page does not send a request to anyone else.

What is stored in your browser

Three tools can save your work in your browser’s local storage so you can come back to it. Each one uses a single named record on your device:

  • The Choosing a College quiz (wcirf_choosing_result_v2) remembers your result, so it reopens where you left off.
  • The College Scorecard (wcirf_choosing_scorecard_v1), a separate tool page, saves the college names, ratings, study notes and leading choice you enter there.
  • The one-semester plan on the In College page (wcirf_in_college_plan_v1) saves the courses, activities, dates and check-ins you write in the plan.

What you type — college names, quiz answers, ratings, notes, courses, activities and dates — stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and clearing your browser data removes it.

These records are local storage, not cookies. Your browser keeps them on your device and does not send them with requests to the site, so they cannot identify you to the server. Every other page works without saving anything. No tool asks for an email address, a password or an account.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in the message. We use it to reply and, if you ask us to, to continue the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list without your permission, and we do not share your address with anyone else.

Outside links

Some pages link to other sites: MIT Press for the book, the US Department of Education’s College Scorecard and Federal Student Aid, and the organizations named in the research. When you follow a link, the other site may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those sites.

A link is not an endorsement, and it is not a tracking arrangement. This site does not embed third-party video or widgets.

Your choices

  • You can clear the site’s local storage at any time through your browser settings.
  • You can browse with cookies blocked, scripts disabled or a content blocker on. The reading pages will still work; the two saving tools will not remember your answers.
  • You can ask us what we have received from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Because we keep so little, the answer is usually simple.

Children

The site is written for adults making decisions about college, and for the people who advise them. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If our practices change — for example, if measurement is added later — we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect. We will not quietly broaden what we collect.
