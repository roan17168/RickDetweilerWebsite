A calmer, clearer What College Is Really For

This is the first stage of the revision: the shared design system, page shell, typography and accessible chart. It exists so we can agree on the craft before the rest of the site moves into it.

Preview only — the live site has not been touched.

The idea

Editorial, not institutional

The old site explained important research, but the presentation often felt like a policy report. The revision keeps the evidence and gives it the calm, confident look of a serious magazine: a strong reading column, a clear hierarchy, generous space and nothing decoration-only.

Readable

Built around the sentence

Lora for headlines and pull-quotes, Inter for the long read. A 720-pixel measure, comfortable line height and no walls of centred text.

Credible

Evidence has a visual language

Navy for findings, gold for the thread that connects them, pale parchment for the page. Charts are drawn from data, not screenshots.

Useful

Every audience gets a door

Students, parents, counselors, faculty, leaders and policymakers each get a clear route in — with the same research underneath.

What the research found

From the national study

Across 1,000 interviews, the same experiences kept predicting a life of consequence, inquiry and accomplishment — and they were not confined to one type of college or one family of majors.

The revision will use this pattern, again and again, to turn a large study into a set of practical questions.

Design note. The old site used 372 inline styles and 50 !important declarations. This system replaces them with tokens and components, so a change happens once.
