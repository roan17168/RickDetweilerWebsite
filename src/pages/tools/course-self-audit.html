Tool

One course, five checks

What it’s for. Look at one course you teach against the five practices in the study, and identify one or two changes to try next term.1 It is for your own use and reflection, not a performance review.

How to use it. Choose one course you teach often, and its format (in person, hybrid, online). Describe what happens now using evidence, rate each practice (Strong, Partway, Not yet), and commit to one change for next term.

Your Faculty check answers are filled into the Rating column.

1. I know students’ names
2. I talk with students outside class
3. Class time is active
4. Assignments have no single right answer
5. Students examine their own thinking

This audit saves on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
