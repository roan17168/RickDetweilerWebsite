Tool

Eight-practice checklist

What it’s for. See how many students get each of the eight practices, who owns each one, and what improving it would cost.

How to use it. Estimate how many students get each experience, name one accountable owner, identify the usual cost of improvement, and choose a next step. Unknown is a finding. Where possible, break results out for commuters, transfers, part-time and first-generation students.

Use Widespread for roughly three-quarters or more, Uneven when access depends on major or individual staff, Rare for roughly one-quarter or fewer, and Unknown when reliable data are unavailable. These are suggested starting points, not standards from the study.

Faculty and staff spend time with students outside class
Every student has at least one mentor
Major requirements leave room for broad study
Courses use discussion, writing, open-ended problems and other student-engaging methods
Students complete an internship, research project or substantial experience
Students regularly work with people whose backgrounds and views differ
First-year courses give faculty time and space to know students
Alumni outcomes are known ten and twenty years out
